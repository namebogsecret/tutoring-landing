FAQ

Questions families ask

Which exams and boards do you cover?

IB Physics (HL/SL), AP Physics (1, 2, C), SAT Maths, A-Level and Cambridge IGCSE (Physics 0625, Maths 0580), plus Edexcel, AQA and OCR. I teach these now and prepare to the exact specification and mark scheme.

How are lessons delivered?

Online 1:1 over Zoom with a shared interactive whiteboard. Based in Yerevan (GMT+4) — convenient for families in the Gulf, Europe, the UK and Asia. Lessons are in English (or bilingual English/Russian if helpful).

What makes your preparation different?

It runs on a structured system, not improvisation: a database of exam syllabi with the real weight of every question type, a week-by-week plan to the exam date, a live progress tracker, and a monthly written report to parents with a grade forecast. The forecast assumes the plan is followed — it's a map, not a promise.

How do payments work?

In USD or EUR via Payoneer or a secure card payment link. We confirm the method on the consultation before the first lesson.

What happens on the free consultation?

A 20-minute call to understand the goal, the current level and the timeline. If it's a fit, I propose a plan and a package; if not, I'll point you in the right direction. No obligation.

Do you only take exam students?

Exam preparation is the focus, but I also coach strong students aiming at olympiads and competitive university entrance.
