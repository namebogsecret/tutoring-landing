What is IB Physics HL? IB Physics Higher Level (HL) is a two-year Diploma Programme course of about 240 teaching hours, assessed on a 1–7 scale across Paper 1 (multiple choice), Paper 2 (data and extended response), Paper 3 (options and experimental work) plus the Internal Assessment. HL covers the same core topics as Standard Level but adds extra depth (AHL material) and more demanding problem solving — which is exactly where structured, exam-targeted preparation moves a grade.
