IB Physics HL tutor online

Vladimir Podlevskikh, an MSU-trained physicist with 23 years of private tutoring and 350+ students, teaches IB Physics HL and SL online, one to one, in English or Russian. HL means 240 hours, a 4 h 30 min external assessment (80%) and an internal assessment (20%).

Key facts

  • Source: IB Physics guide, first assessment 2025; five topics A–E
  • HL: 240 hours (180 of content); SL: 150 hours (110 of content)
  • HL Paper 1: 2 h, 60 marks, 36% (40 multiple-choice + data-based questions); Paper 2: 2 h 30 min, 90 marks, 44%
  • Internal assessment: 20%, one scientific investigation, 24 marks, same for SL and HL
  • Calculators allowed; Physics data booklet required; no marks deducted for wrong multiple-choice answers

What HL adds to SL

HL covers the SL material plus additional HL content: topic D (Fields), for example, is 38 hours at HL and 19 at SL. Paper 2 at HL contains the SL material plus extra HL questions.

Internal assessment

The IA is assessed on Research design, Data analysis, Conclusion and Evaluation. The teacher reads one draft and advises but does not edit, so lessons build the method and the data handling; the student writes the work.
