Russian-speaking AP Physics tutor online

AP Physics preparation online in Russian is led by Vladimir Podlevskikh, an MSU physicist: one-to-one lessons, explanations in Russian, College Board tasks and terms in English. Below is the AP Physics C exam format.

Key facts

  • AP Physics C is a college-level, calculus-based course
  • Format (per College Board, for the May 2027 exam): Section I — 42 multiple-choice questions, 85 minutes, 50%; Section II — 4 free-response questions, 95 minutes, 50%
  • Calculators are allowed
  • Mechanics: 7 units; Electricity and Magnetism: 6 units
  • Lesson language: Russian, English or mixed

Units

Mechanics: kinematics; force and translational dynamics; work, energy and power; linear momentum; torque and rotational dynamics; energy and momentum of rotating systems; oscillations. Electricity and Magnetism: electric charges, fields and Gauss's law; electric potential; conductors and capacitors; electric circuits; magnetic fields; electromagnetic induction.

What we do in lessons

We work through the units, practise the four free-response question types (mathematical derivations, translation between representations, experimental design, qualitative-quantitative problems) and sit timed full papers.
