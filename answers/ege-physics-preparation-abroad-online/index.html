What changes abroad

The main problem is the gap between the local programme and the EGE. Topics, order and task format differ, so the first step is to match what has been covered against the codifier and plan how to close the gaps. After that the work is the same as for in-person preparation: task types, extended solutions against the criteria, full 235-minute papers.

Related programmes

If the student also follows IB, AP or IGCSE, this is taken into account in the overall plan: see the IB Physics, AP Physics and IGCSE Physics 0625 pages below.

10-minute diagnostic

Five questions on the topic of this page, one correct answer out of four each. The result appears once all five are answered; it is a guide to core topics, not a prediction of an exam grade.

  1. The net force on a 2 kg body is 6 N. What is its acceleration?

  2. An ideal gas is heated at constant volume so that its absolute temperature doubles. What happens to the pressure?

  3. A voltage of 5 V is applied across a 10 ohm resistor. What power is dissipated in it?

  4. A body is thrown vertically upward at 20 m/s. Ignore air resistance and take g = 10 m/s². What maximum height does it reach?

  5. The frequency of the light in the photoelectric effect is increased (above the threshold) with the intensity unchanged. What happens to the maximum kinetic energy of the photoelectrons?
