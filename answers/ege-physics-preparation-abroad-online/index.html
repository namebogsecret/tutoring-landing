EGE physics preparation abroad, online

You can prepare for the EGE in physics entirely online from any country: lessons are one-to-one, the time is fitted to the family's time zone, and the programme follows the EGE 2027 specification. Led by MSU physicist Vladimir Podlevskikh, 23 years of teaching.

Key facts

  • Format: online, 1:1, worldwide (GMT+2/+3)
  • Language: Russian or English
  • If the child follows an international programme, its topics are first matched against the EGE codifier
  • Exam: 26 tasks, 45 primary points, 235 minutes
  • The DVI and olympiads can be prepared alongside the EGE

What changes abroad

The main problem is the gap between the local programme and the EGE. Topics, order and task format differ, so the first step is to match what has been covered against the codifier and plan how to close the gaps. After that the work is the same as for in-person preparation: task types, extended solutions against the criteria, full 235-minute papers.

Related programmes

If the student also follows IB, AP or IGCSE, this is taken into account in the overall plan: see the IB Physics, AP Physics and IGCSE Physics 0625 pages below.

Does online preparation work if the family lives in another time zone? +
Yes. Lesson times are fitted to the family's time zone; lessons run online worldwide (GMT+2/+3).
Where to start if the child followed an international programme? +
By matching the topics of that course against the EGE codifier: this shows which sections are already covered and which remain.
Can lessons be in English? +
Yes. Lessons are in Russian or English, whichever suits the student.
