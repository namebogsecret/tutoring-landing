Answer · IGCSE Physics 0625 tutor Russian-speaking

Russian-speaking IGCSE Physics 0625 tutor

Yes: Vladimir Podlevskikh, an MSU-trained physicist, teaches Cambridge IGCSE Physics 0625 online, one to one, in Russian, English or a mix: explanations in Russian, exam terminology and command words in English, as on the papers.

Key facts

  • Syllabus: Cambridge IGCSE Physics 0625, examined 2026, 2027 and 2028; six topics
  • Core: Papers 1 + 3 + practical (grades C–G); Extended: Papers 2 + 4 + practical (grades A*–G)
  • Practical: Paper 5 (1 h 15 min, 40 marks) or Paper 6 (1 h, 40 marks), 20% of the grade
  • Assessment objectives: AO1 50%, AO2 30%, AO3 20%
  • Format: online, 1:1, worldwide (GMT+2/+3)

Why Russian helps

A student coming from a Russian-language physics course usually knows the ideas but not the English wording. Lessons connect the two: the concept is explained in Russian, then the same answer is written the way the IGCSE paper expects.

Core or Extended

Core suits candidates expecting a D or below; Extended suits those expecting a C or higher. The entry is decided together with the family's educational institution, because the two tiers are prepared differently.

Who teaches

Vladimir Podlevskikh is a physicist, a graduate of the Faculty of Physics of Lomonosov Moscow State University (2006), with 23 years of private tutoring, 350+ students and a perfect 200/200 on the US Praxis Physics exam (5266).

FAQ

Common questions

Can IGCSE Physics 0625 be taught in Russian? +
Yes. Lessons can be in Russian, English or a mix, with exam terminology in English.
What are the six IGCSE Physics topics? +
Motion, forces and energy; Thermal physics; Waves; Electricity and magnetism; Nuclear physics; Space physics.
Is IGCSE Physics the same as the Russian physics course? +
No: the order and weighting of topics differ, and practical skills are a separate 20% of the grade. A diagnostic across the six topics shows what is already covered.
