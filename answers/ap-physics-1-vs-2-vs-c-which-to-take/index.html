AP Physics 1 vs 2 vs C: which exam to take

AP Physics 1 and 2 are algebra-based introductory courses: Physics 1 covers mechanics and fluids, Physics 2 heat, electricity, magnetism, optics and modern physics. AP Physics C is calculus-based. All the exams share one format: 42 multiple-choice questions in 85 minutes and 4 free-response questions in 95 minutes. Updated: 15 November 2026

Key facts

  • Source: College Board AP Central; the format figures are stated as effective starting with the May 2027 exam
  • AP Physics 1 — 8 units (kinematics, force and translational dynamics, work, energy and power, linear momentum, torque and rotational dynamics, energy and momentum of rotating systems, oscillations, fluids)
  • AP Physics 2 — 7 units (9–15): thermodynamics; electric force, field and potential; electric circuits; magnetism and electromagnetism; geometric optics; waves, sound and physical optics; modern physics
  • AP Physics C: Mechanics — 7 units, E&M — 6 units; separate exams
  • Section I — 42 questions, 85 minutes, 50%; Section II — 4 questions, 95 minutes, 50%; calculators permitted
  • Lessons: online, 1:1, English, Russian or mixed

How the courses differ

The main difference is the mathematics. College Board describes Physics 1 and 2 as algebra-based and Physics C as calculus-based. The seven mechanics units of Physics 1 and of Physics C: Mechanics have the same titles (kinematics, force and translational dynamics, work, energy and power, linear momentum, torque and rotational dynamics, energy and momentum of rotating systems, oscillations); Physics 1 adds a fluids unit, while Physics C is a calculus-based course. Physics 2 continues with heat, electricity, magnetism, optics, waves and modern physics. E&M in Physics C is a separate exam on electricity and magnetism.

Which to take

The choice depends on the mathematics already mastered, the courses available at the student's own institution and the requirements of the programme applied to. College Board does not list prerequisites on the course pages, so the decision is made together with the institution and after a diagnostic. Mechanics without calculus: Physics 1; electricity, optics and modern physics without calculus: Physics 2; physics with calculus: Physics C, where Mechanics and E&M are chosen separately.

How to prepare

Preparation follows the format, which is the same for all four exams: a multiple-choice section and four free-response question types (mathematical routines, translation between representations, experimental design and analysis, qualitative/quantitative translation). The routine: a diagnostic by unit, a plan weighted by the unit shares of the multiple-choice section, regular free-response practice, timed full papers and error review. The Physics 1 and 2 exams are hybrid digital: multiple-choice is taken in the Bluebook app and free-response answers are handwritten in paper booklets, so handwritten solution layout is practised separately.

Official pages

Units, weights and the current format are on the College Board pages: AP Physics 1, AP Physics 2, AP Physics C: Mechanics, AP Physics C: E&M. The format can change; rely on AP Central.

FAQ

Common questions

What is the difference between AP Physics 1 and AP Physics 2? +
Content: Physics 1 covers mechanics and fluids (8 units); Physics 2 covers thermodynamics, electricity, magnetism, optics, waves and modern physics (7 units). Both are algebra-based.
How does AP Physics C differ from AP Physics 1? +
Physics C is a calculus-based college-level course; Physics 1 is an algebra-based introductory one. Mechanics in Physics 1 and in Physics C: Mechanics runs through units with the same titles; the courses differ in mathematics (algebra-based vs calculus-based).
What is the AP Physics exam format? +
Section I: 42 multiple-choice questions, 85 minutes, 50% of the score; Section II: 4 free-response questions, 95 minutes, 50%. Calculators are permitted. Figures per College Board: the format is effective starting with the May 2027 exam.
Can I prepare for AP Physics in Russian? +
Yes. Explanations in Russian, College Board wording and terms in English; fully in English is possible too.
Which AP Physics course should I choose? +
It depends on the mathematics already mastered, the courses available and the requirements of the programme applied to. We sort it out in a diagnostic; College Board does not list prerequisites on its course pages.
