How to choose an AP Physics 1 tutor online: 5 criteria

Choose an AP Physics 1 tutor by five checks: the 8-unit algebra-based course, the real exam format, free-response coaching, a plan by unit weights, sources instead of promised scores. Vladimir Podlevskikh, an MSU physicist, teaches it online, 1:1.

Updated: 15 November 2026

Key facts

  • Source: College Board AP Central, AP Physics 1 course and exam pages (checked 15 November 2026)
  • AP Physics 1 is an algebra-based, introductory college-level physics course with 8 units
  • Exam format, effective starting with the May 2027 exam: Section I has 42 multiple-choice questions (85 min, 50%); Section II has 4 free-response questions (95 min, 50%)
  • Calculators are allowed; in the Physics 1 and 2 exams multiple-choice is taken in Bluebook and free-response by hand in a paper booklet
  • Four free-response types: mathematical routines; translation between representations; experimental design and analysis; qualitative/quantitative translation

The checklist: five criteria

  1. The right course. AP Physics 1 covers 8 units: Kinematics; Force and Translational Dynamics; Work, Energy, and Power; Linear Momentum; Torque and Rotational Dynamics; Energy and Momentum of Rotating Systems; Oscillations; Fluids. A tutor should name them without hesitation and keep to algebra-based methods.
  2. The exam format. Half of the exam is 42 multiple-choice questions in 85 minutes, half is 4 free-response questions in 95 minutes. Ask how often lessons include timed sections in this format.
  3. Free-response skills. The four free-response questions are one of each type listed above. A good tutor teaches how to write a justified solution, not only how to reach a number.
  4. A plan by unit weights. The unit shares in the multiple-choice section differ: Force and Translational Dynamics and Work, Energy, and Power are 18–23% each, Torque and Rotational Dynamics 10–15%, Oscillations 5–8%. The plan should follow a diagnostic, not a fixed template.
  5. A process you can check. Homework between lessons, a written error review, and a source for each statement about the exam.

What a tutor cannot honestly promise

Score conversion, exam dates and university credit are set by College Board and by each university and change over time, so a tutor who promises a particular score or a fixed credit outcome is giving information nobody can verify. Check dates and requirements on AP Central and with the programme you apply to.

How to test a tutor in one lesson

Bring one free-response question from a past exam and ask the tutor to solve it aloud. Watch for three things: whether the reasoning is written step by step, whether the tutor names the question type, and whether the lesson ends with a short list of the topics to revisit.

Official sources

The course and exam description is on the AP Physics 1 page at apcentral.collegeboard.org. Format details apply from the May 2027 exam; check the page for later updates.

FAQ

Common questions

How do I choose an AP Physics 1 tutor? +
Check five things: the tutor knows the 8-unit algebra-based course, practises the real exam format, coaches free-response writing, plans lessons by unit weights after a diagnostic, and gives sources for exam facts.
What is the AP Physics 1 exam format? +
Section I: 42 multiple-choice questions, 85 minutes, 50% of the exam. Section II: 4 free-response questions, 95 minutes, 50%. Calculators are allowed. This applies starting with the May 2027 exam.
Should a tutor teach AP Physics 1 with calculus? +
AP Physics 1 is described by College Board as an algebra-based course, so lessons should use algebra-based methods. Calculus-based material belongs to AP Physics C.
What should a good first lesson include? +
A short diagnostic by the 8 units, one free-response question solved with written reasoning, and a draft plan weighted by the unit shares.
Can I prepare for AP Physics 1 in Russian? +
Yes: explanations in Russian with exam terminology and wording in English, or fully in English.
