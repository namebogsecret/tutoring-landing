Russian-speaking IB Physics tutor online

IB Physics (SL and HL) can be studied with a Russian-speaking teacher: Vladimir Podlevskikh, an MSU physicist, teaches online, one-to-one, explaining in Russian and working with IB terms and tasks in English.

Key facts

  • Programme: IB Physics guide, first assessment 2025; 5 topics (A–E)
  • Hours: SL — 150, HL — 240
  • External assessment — 80%: SL 3 h, HL 4 h 30 min
  • Internal assessment (IA) — 20%: investigation, 24 marks, criteria Research design, Data analysis, Conclusion, Evaluation
  • Lesson language: Russian, English or mixed

Course topics

Five topics: A — space, time and motion; B — the particulate nature of matter; C — wave behaviour; D — fields; E — nuclear and quantum physics. HL is larger in scope: for example, topic D takes 38 hours against 19 at SL.

Exam papers

SL: Paper 1 — 1 h 30 min, 45 marks (25 multiple-choice questions and data-based tasks); Paper 2 — 1 h 30 min, 50 marks. HL: Paper 1 — 2 h, 60 marks (40 multiple-choice questions and data-based tasks); Paper 2 — 2 h 30 min, 90 marks. No marks are deducted for a wrong multiple-choice answer. The Physics data booklet is required; calculators are allowed.

IA

The teacher reads one draft and gives advice but does not edit the work, so lessons practise the investigation method, data processing and evaluation of uncertainties, while the student writes the text.

Who teaches

Vladimir Podlevskikh is a physicist, a graduate of the Faculty of Physics of Lomonosov Moscow State University (2006), with 23 years of private tutoring, 350+ students and a perfect 200/200 on the US Praxis Physics exam (5266).

FAQ

Common questions

Can I study IB Physics in Russian? +
Yes: explanations in Russian, IB terms and task wording in English, whichever suits the student.
What is the difference between IB Physics SL and HL? +
Scope and depth: SL is 150 hours, HL 240; external assessment is 3 hours at SL and 4 hours 30 minutes at HL. The IA is the same for both levels.
Do you help with the Internal Assessment? +
Yes, with the method, the data and assessment against the criteria; the student writes the work.
