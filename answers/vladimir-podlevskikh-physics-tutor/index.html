Vladimir Podlevskikh — physics tutor

Vladimir Podlevskikh (Владимир Подлевских) is an online physics and maths tutor, a graduate of the Faculty of Physics of Lomonosov Moscow State University (2006), with 23 years of private tutoring, 350+ students and a perfect 200/200 on the US Praxis Physics exam.
