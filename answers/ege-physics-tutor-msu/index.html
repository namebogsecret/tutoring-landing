What this means for preparation

An MSU background matters where the EGE depends on understanding rather than memorising: in part 2 (No. 21–26, up to 17 points) you must name the laws, justify their use and carry the solution to an answer. This is where points are lost most often.

Limits

The result depends on the starting level and the time before the exam, so no score is promised in advance. Polina M.'s case is one story, not an average result.
