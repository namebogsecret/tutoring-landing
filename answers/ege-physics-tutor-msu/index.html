What this means for preparation

An MSU background matters where the EGE depends on understanding rather than memorising: in part 2 (No. 21–26, up to 17 points) you must name the laws, justify their use and carry the solution to an answer. This is where points are lost most often.

Limits

The result depends on the starting level and the time before the exam, so no score is promised in advance. Polina M.'s case is one story, not an average result.

10-minute diagnostic

Five questions on the topic of this page, one correct answer out of four each. Answers can be changed; the button shows the result once all five are answered; it is a guide to core topics, not a prediction of an exam grade.

  1. The net force on a 2 kg body is 6 N. What is its acceleration?

  2. An ideal gas is heated at constant volume so that its absolute temperature doubles. What happens to the pressure?

  3. A voltage of 5 V is applied across a 10 ohm resistor. What power is dissipated in it?

  4. A body is thrown vertically upward at 20 m/s. Ignore air resistance and take g = 10 m/s². What maximum height does it reach?

  5. The frequency of the light in the photoelectric effect is increased (above the threshold) with the intensity unchanged. What happens to the maximum kinetic energy of the photoelectrons?
