IB Physics HL vs SL: differences and how to prepare

HL and SL are one course at different depth: 240 hours against 150, external assessment 4 h 30 min against 3 h, and wider topics (topic D, Fields, is 38 hours at HL and 19 at SL). The internal assessment is the same: 20% of the grade, 24 marks. Vladimir Podlevskikh, an MSU physicist, prepares students for IB Physics online, one to one.

Updated: 15 November 2026

Key facts

  • Source: IB Physics guide, first assessment 2025; five topics A–E
  • SL: 150 hours (110 of content); HL: 240 hours (180 of content)
  • SL: Paper 1 — 1 h 30 min, 45 marks; Paper 2 — 1 h 30 min, 50 marks
  • HL: Paper 1 — 2 h, 60 marks; Paper 2 — 2 h 30 min, 90 marks
  • Internal assessment: 20%, one investigation, 24 marks, same for SL and HL
  • Calculators allowed; Physics data booklet required; no marks deducted for wrong multiple-choice answers

How HL and SL differ

SL material is part of HL: HL Paper 2 contains the SL material plus additional HL questions. The difference is breadth and depth in each of the five topics, shown in the table. Paper 1 has the same structure at both levels: part 1A with multiple-choice questions and part 1B with data-based questions.

Which to take

The level (HL or SL) is chosen together with the student's own institution and depends on the requirements of the programme applied to; those requirements are checked with the programme itself. A diagnostic by topics A–E shows whether the HL volume is realistic.

How to prepare

The routine: a diagnostic by topics A–E, a plan weighted by the hours per topic, practice of Paper 1 (multiple-choice and data-based) and of the Paper 2 extended answers, timed mock papers and error review. Separately, work with the Physics data booklet and the guide's command terms, which decide how a question is read. The internal assessment starts early: the teacher reads one draft and advises but does not edit, so lessons build the research method, data handling, uncertainty evaluation and the criteria Research design, Data analysis, Conclusion and Evaluation, while the student writes the work.

Official document

The current description is in the Physics guide (first assessment 2025) on ibo.org; check programme updates and dates there and with the student's institution.

FAQ

Common questions

What is the difference between IB Physics HL and SL? +
Breadth and depth: HL is 240 hours with 4 h 30 min of external assessment, SL is 150 hours with 3 h. The topics (A–E) are the same, wider at HL; the internal assessment is identical.
How long are the IB Physics exams? +
SL: Paper 1 is 1 h 30 min, Paper 2 is 1 h 30 min. HL: Paper 1 is 2 h, Paper 2 is 2 h 30 min.
How should I prepare for IB Physics? +
A diagnostic by topics A–E, a plan by topic hours, Paper 1 and Paper 2 practice in the exam format, timed mock papers and an early start on the internal assessment.
Is the internal assessment the same for HL and SL? +
Yes: one scientific investigation, 24 marks, 20% of the grade; the teacher reads one draft and advises but does not edit.
Can I prepare for IB Physics in Russian? +
Yes: explanations in Russian with IB terms and wording in English, or fully in English.
