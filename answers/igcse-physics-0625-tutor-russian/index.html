IGCSE Physics 0625 tutor who speaks Russian

Cambridge IGCSE Physics 0625 can be studied in Russian: Vladimir Podlevskikh, an MSU physicist, explains the material in Russian and gives exam terms and wording in English, as in the Cambridge syllabus. Lessons are online, one-to-one.

Key facts

  • Programme code: Cambridge IGCSE Physics 0625, syllabus 2026–2028
  • 6 topics: motion, forces and energy; thermal physics; waves; electricity and magnetism; nuclear physics; space physics
  • Core: Paper 1 + Paper 3 + practical, grades C–G; Extended: Paper 2 + Paper 4 + practical, grades A*–G
  • The practical component is 20% of the mark (Paper 5 or Paper 6)
  • Lesson language: Russian, English or mixed

Core or Extended

Core leads to grades C–G and suits those expecting D and below; Extended (Core + Supplement) leads to grades A*–G, for those expecting C and above. The level is chosen together with the student's own institution at the start of preparation, because the two are prepared differently.

What the mark consists of

Assessment objectives: knowledge with understanding — 50%, handling information and problem solving — 30%, experimental skills — 20%. Half of the marks are for applying physics to data and problems, so lessons use exam-style questions.

Who teaches

FAQ

Common questions

Can I study IGCSE Physics in Russian? +
Yes. Explanations are in Russian, exam terminology and wording in English, whichever suits the student.
What is the difference between Paper 5 and Paper 6? +
Each gives 20% of the mark. Paper 5 is a practical test (1 h 15 min, 40 marks), Paper 6 is an alternative to practical (1 hour, 40 marks); one of them is taken.
How many hours does the IGCSE Physics course take? +
Cambridge assumes about 130 guided learning hours; this is an estimate of the course size, not a norm for tutoring.
