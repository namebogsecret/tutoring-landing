How to choose an IB Physics HL tutor online: 5 criteria

Choose an IB Physics HL tutor by five checks: the current guide (topics A–E), separate Paper 1 and 2 practice, command terms and data booklet, internal assessment guided not written, a plan by topic hours. Vladimir Podlevskikh, an MSU physicist, teaches it online.

Updated: 15 November 2026

Key facts

  • Source: IB Physics guide, first assessment 2025; five topics A–E
  • HL: 240 hours (180 of content); SL: 150 hours (110 of content)
  • HL external assessment, 4 h 30 min: Paper 1 — 2 h, 60 marks; Paper 2 — 2 h 30 min, 90 marks
  • Internal assessment: 20%, one scientific investigation, 24 marks; the teacher reads one draft and advises but does not edit
  • Calculators allowed; Physics data booklet required; no marks deducted for wrong multiple-choice answers

The checklist: five criteria

  1. The current guide. The tutor works from the Physics guide (first assessment 2025): topics A Space, time and motion; B The particulate nature of matter; C Wave behaviour; D Fields; E Nuclear and quantum physics, with the additional HL material on top of SL.
  2. Paper 1 and Paper 2 separately. Paper 1 has part 1A (40 multiple-choice questions) and part 1B (data-based questions); Paper 2 is short-answer and extended-response and includes the additional HL material. They need different practice, in timed conditions.
  3. Command terms and the data booklet. Calculators are allowed and the Physics data booklet is required, so lessons should include work with the booklet and with the guide's command terms.
  4. Help with the internal assessment. The IA is 20% of the grade and the teacher reads one draft and does not edit it. A tutor helps with research design, data analysis, conclusion and evaluation, while the student writes the work.
  5. A plan by topic hours. At HL the hours per topic are A 42, B 32, C 29, D 38, E 39. The plan should follow a diagnostic by topics A–E and weight time accordingly.

Where a tutor helps in each part of the course

Paper 1: timed multiple-choice sets and data-based questions, with error review by topic. Paper 2: written extended answers and the HL-only material. Internal assessment: method and data handling against the four criteria. Across all parts: reading questions through command terms.

Questions to ask a tutor before the first lesson

How do you split the 240 HL hours across topics A–E? How are Paper 1 and Paper 2 practised differently? What exactly will you do and not do for my internal assessment? A tutor who answers concretely and points to the guide is easier to trust than one who answers in general terms.

The current description is in the Physics guide (first assessment 2025) on ibo.org; check programme updates and dates there and with the student's institution.

FAQ

Common questions

How do I choose an IB Physics HL tutor? +
Check five things: the tutor works from the current guide with topics A–E, practises Paper 1 and Paper 2 separately, uses command terms and the data booklet, helps with the internal assessment without writing it, and plans by topic hours.
How does a tutor help with the IB Physics internal assessment? +
By building the method: research design, data analysis, conclusion and evaluation. The investigation itself is written by the student; the teacher reads one draft and advises but does not edit.
How long are the IB Physics HL papers? +
Paper 1 is 2 h (60 marks) and Paper 2 is 2 h 30 min (90 marks); together the external assessment is 4 h 30 min.
Is there a Paper 3 in IB Physics HL? +
No — in the Physics guide (first assessment 2025), the HL external assessment is made up of Paper 1 (2 h) and Paper 2 (2 h 30 min), which together give 4 h 30 min.
Can I prepare for IB Physics HL in Russian? +
Yes: explanations in Russian with IB terms and wording in English, or fully in English.
